Welcome to JJC Cyber Wolves Club

A venue for students to collaborate, exchange ideas, knowledge, and skills on various cybersecurity aspects.

About Our Club

The JJC Cyber Wolves Club is for those interested in Information Technology or Cybersecurity. The primary focus of the club is to learn the skills necessary to enter the cybersecurity field. Members will have the opportunity to prepare and participate in cybersecurity capture the flag competitions, attend cybersecurity conferences, workshops, career planning, and professional certifications training.
